## Top 15 rules

1. **Own what AI builds.** Use AI to build an asset you own, not to do work for an employer or client; ask "when AI finishes the work, whose is it?" (01-opp ~01:45)
2. **Build assets, not hours (stop test).** If the money stops when you stop, it's a job; a digital product is built once and sold many times (01-opp ~01:49, ~02:18; 02a-vip ~00:11)
3. **A digital product follows two rules:** developed digitally and delivered digitally; the format matters less than whether it solves a problem (01-opp ~01:48; 02a-vip ~00:09). Buyers care about the problem and the fix, not the creator (**intrinsic value**), which is why faceless works (03a-vip ~00:06–00:13)
4. **The product decides everything.** The wrong product sells nothing even in front of 50,000 ready buyers (02b-main ~01:08)
5. **Start from demand, not passion:** pick a problem people already search for, already pay for, and can't solve well yet (02b-main ~01:26, ~01:33)
6. **Generate past your first ideas** and prefer proven, simple problems over new inventions (02b-main ~01:29; 02a-vip ~00:23)
7. **Solve a pain, not a pleasure**, in wealth, health or relationships; the best pains are felt daily and get worse fast (02a-vip ~00:25–00:31)
8. **Go specific, but not too narrow:** a specific person with a specific problem, narrowed by a real distinguishing trait (not "busy"), yet common enough that plenty of people have it; a few dozen to a few hundred buyers is enough (02a-vip ~00:18, ~00:46–00:47; 02b-main ~01:30–01:31)
9. **Sell the transformation, not the content.** Map point A (feel, fear, avoid, self-image) first, then point B (02a-vip ~00:35–00:38)
10. **Describe their problem better than they can**: that recognition is what sells (02a-vip ~00:39, ~00:46)
11. **Write a four-part product promise:** "I help [who] with [worsening problem] get [result] in [time]" (02a-vip ~00:51)
12. **Don't invent; synthesise.** Merge 150+ existing sources into one step-by-step method, present it as what the experts agree on, and include the references (02b-main ~01:35–01:36)
13. **Shorter and faster beats bigger:** nobody pays for volume; ship a simple ebook MVP (~45-page PDF) first and add complexity only once simple sells (02b-main ~01:41, ~01:45; 02a-vip ~00:15, ~00:49); get market feedback fast and commit only once it sells (04a-vip ~00:27)
14. **Iterate and build a portfolio:** expect $0 from the first products; keep building until some earn $15, $50, $100 a day (02b-main ~01:48)
15. **Distribute and automate instead of selling:** place the product where presold buyers already gather (groups of 10k–100k) and deliver automatically so 10 or 10,000 sales take the same work (01-opp ~02:15, ~02:21, ~02:24). The course's method: partner with **micro-creators (10k–100k followers) who have no product**, personalise the product to them, publish with an automatic revenue split (03b-main ~01:25, ~01:31)
