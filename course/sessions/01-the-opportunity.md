## Summary
Why AI digital products: if you use AI at your job, the employer keeps the upside.
To earn from AI you must **own what it builds**. AI digital products (PDFs, guides,
templates, Notion templates, e-books) are built once and sold many times, need no
capital, and you don't have to show your face. Keep your job, start on the side.

## Key ideas
- **3D system:** Develop → Distribute → Deliver (the whole week builds on this)
- **Stop test:** "If I stop, does the money stop?" If yes, it's a second job, not an asset
- **Turning Point:** the day side income overtakes main income
- Small numbers can work: 37 buyers × $250/month ≈ $9k/month (Fortnite maps guide)
- Narrow problems sell: woodworking plans, Pokémon cards, buckwheat diet, US credit cards for non-US citizens
- Success habits: rewatch what's unclear instead of quitting; have a strong why
