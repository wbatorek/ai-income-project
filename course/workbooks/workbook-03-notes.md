## What's new or clearer than in the session
- **Five filters, not four** (p.19–20): views 50k–200k on a video · 10k–100k followers · no own product ·
  **average Reels views (last 10) ≥ followers ÷ 10** · business email in bio.
- **Creator market (p.2, p.11):** 303M creators; 41M micro-creators on Instagram; <1% have a digital
  product; Linktree: 46% of full-time and 68% of part-time creators earn under $1,000/year.
- **Deals (p.13–14):** James 30/70 split plus $6,000 upfront; Gaël's creator had 300k TikTok followers
  (outside the band) and he now runs 4 partnerships for ~$10k/month.
- **The offer now adds** ListKit with 100 pre-loaded leads and Synthesise's "hidden feature" (creator's
  Instagram link → personalised product) (p.18, p.29).
- **"Done for you" claims (p.17):** outreach, building the product and getting paid. ListKit is said to
  send "tens of thousands of emails a day" (p.20).
  > Our note: mass cold email has legal limits (CAN-SPAM, GDPR/ePrivacy, PECR). See requirement R12a-3.
