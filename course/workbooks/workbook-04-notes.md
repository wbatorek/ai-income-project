## The five secrets (as written in the workbook)
1. **You never feel ready.** Limiting beliefs survive by keeping you away from the evidence; test them.
   Mark: rejected by one bank at 20, walked into the next and got £20,000, paid back in under two years.
2. **Being wrong vs being early.** "What if I just don't understand it yet?" In 2004 he built a simple
   website as a test; within weeks people were buying online. "When nobody gets it, that's the signal."
3. **The real risk never sends a bill.** Two risks: doing something (visible) and doing nothing
   (invisible). One income = a one-legged stool. 1988: £20k loan, 10-year lease, stock, staff. Today:
   a laptop, an internet connection, a few evenings.
4. **The biggest returns are on the smallest bets.** Buffett's ~20%/year turns $10k into ~$25k in five
   years; small bets (a $150 lawnmower, $25 a lawn) give asymmetric returns big players can't access.
   The pitch: the best investment is in yourself.
5. **Knowing was never the hard part. Doing is.** A friend stole his business plan; Mark later bought
   the shop and turned it around: "having the same idea doesn't mean you'll get the same result".
   **"Real research has a purpose, and eventually it has an end."**

> Our note: secret 5's research rule is the useful takeaway for us: phase 1 needs an end date.
