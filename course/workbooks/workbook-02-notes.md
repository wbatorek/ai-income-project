## Course questions answered from the material
- **Profitable pocket vs broad topic:** a pocket is a *specific person with a specific problem*
  (postpartum weight loss for new mothers, not "weight loss"); a broad topic is a category with
  heavy competition. (p.9)
- **Example pocket:** business English for non-native executives; quick decision-making
  frameworks for first-time entrepreneurs. (p.9)
- **Specialised vs generic AI (as the course claims):** "generic" AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
  does a bit of everything; "specialised" AI is built for one job and handles 150+ sources.
  > Our note: this is a sales argument. Current Claude handles large source sets fine; see
  > [02b-develop §5](../sessions/02b-develop.md).
- **Why you don't need to be an expert:** the product presents what the experts agree on, from
  150+ cited sources. You assemble, you don't invent. (p.10)
- **Why a 45-page PDF beats a 12-hour course:** "nobody pays for volume, they pay for a problem
  going away". It's fast to consume and fast to build. (p.11)
- **Progression:** $15 + $50 + $100 per day = $165/day ≈ **$5,000/month**. (p.13)
