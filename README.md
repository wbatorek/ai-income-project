# ai-income-project

My personal system for building AI digital products, created while following the
AI Income Challenge (Nov 7–16, 2026).

## Framework: the 3D system
1. **Develop** – create the product with AI
2. **Distribute** – put it where buyers already gather (communities of 10k–100k)
3. **Deliver** – automated delivery (Whop), so 10 or 10,000 sales take the same effort

## Structure
- `sessions/` – notes and tasks from each live session
- `workbooks/` – filled-in challenge workbooks
- `research/` – niche and product research
- `products/` – one folder per product (idea, outline, assets, launch plan)

## My why
A stable income that doesn't depend on my hours, so I can work less and have
more time for friends, family and travel.
