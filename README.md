# ai-income-project

## Goal

**Make a lot of money selling digital products, using an AI system that finds niches, creates
products and sells them, mostly on its own.**

The system does the work. The operator (me) approves key decisions: which niches to enter, what
gets published, what gets posted in public, and anything that spends money.

Success is measured in **revenue that doesn't depend on my hours**. The first milestone is the
*Turning Point*: the day this income overtakes my main income.

## How we get there

1. **Learn the model.** Follow the AI Income Challenge course and capture its knowledge in
   [`course/`](course/).
2. **Do our own research.** Markets, trends, tools, platforms and automation go in
   [`research/`](research/). We don't rely on the course alone.
3. **Design and build the system.** A multi-agent setup with operator approval gates, in
   [`system/`](system/).
4. **Ship products.** Every product we create, test and sell is tracked in [`products/`](products/).

## Where things come from

We keep the source of every piece of knowledge clear:

| Folder | Contains | Source |
|---|---|---|
| [`course/`](course/) | Session notes, workbooks, frameworks taught in the course | **AI Income Challenge** (Iman Gadzhi, Nov 2026) |
| [`research/`](research/) | Market, niche, tool and platform research | **Our own research** |
| [`system/`](system/) | Architecture and design of our AI system | Our design, using both sources |
| [`products/`](products/) | One folder per product: idea, validation, assets, launch, results | Our work |

When a document in `system/` or `products/` relies on something, it links to where it came from,
so we can always tell course claims from independently checked facts.

## The core model: the 3D system (from the course)

1. **Develop:** create the product with AI.
2. **Distribute:** put it where buyers already gather (communities of 10k–100k people).
3. **Deliver:** automated delivery and payment, so 10 sales and 10,000 sales take the same effort.

Our system automates all three, plus the step before them: **finding the right niche**.
